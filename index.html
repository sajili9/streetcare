Frequently Asked Questions

Who can report a street issue?

Any citizen can register and report civic issues through this platform.

Can I upload a photo?

Yes. Images help authorities identify the issue more accurately.

Can I track my complaint?

Yes. Every complaint receives a unique Complaint ID for tracking.

Is registration required?

Yes. Users must create an account before submitting complaints.
