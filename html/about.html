About StreetCare

StreetCare is a proposed digital platform designed to simplify the reporting and tracking of street-related civic issues.

Report an Issue Back to Home

About the Project

A simple digital approach to identifying, reporting and monitoring public infrastructure issues.

Simple Reporting

Citizens can submit information about street problems using a simple online form.

Location Based

The system can capture the location of a reported issue to make identification easier.

Photo Evidence

Users can attach photographs to provide visual information about the reported issue.

Status Tracking

Users can monitor the progress of their submitted complaints using a complaint ID.

The Problem

Street infrastructure problems can affect safety, transportation and quality of life.

Damaged Roads

Potholes and damaged road surfaces can create difficulties for commuters.

Street Lighting

Non-functioning street lights can reduce visibility during nighttime.

Waste Problems

Improperly managed waste can affect cleanliness and public spaces.

Drainage Issues

Blocked drainage systems can contribute to waterlogging during heavy rainfall.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

Make civic issue reporting simple, accessible and organized.

Vision

Encourage citizens to participate in creating safer and cleaner communities.

Citizens

Give citizens a simple channel to communicate local infrastructure issues.

Smart City

Support data-driven management of reported civic issues.

Project Objectives

The system is designed around the following objectives.

Easy Submission

Provide a simple interface for submitting street-related complaints.

Centralized Records

Maintain organized records of submitted complaints and their status.

Easy Tracking

Allow users to check the current status of their submitted issue.

Efficient Management

Provide administrators with a dashboard for managing reported issues.

How StreetCare Works

The proposed system follows a simple reporting workflow.

1

Register

Create an account to access the reporting system.

2

Submit

Enter the issue details, location and supporting image.

3

Process

The administrator reviews and updates the complaint.

4

Resolve

The issue status is updated after appropriate action.
